## Segment 2: The Campfire
*Where did this all really start?*

Talk about: Colorado, 2016, elk hunting with Brad. Sitting at elevation around a campfire, elk bugling through mountain mist. The concept of pitchforks — giving people tools for change that don't require destruction. Years of voracious input finally starting to take shape. The natural evolution — tucked aside, revisited, forgotten, remembered.

## Segment 3: Pitchforks Without the Carnage
*What's the problem you're trying to solve?*

Talk about: pitchforks as an abstraction for when people have had enough. The idea of a more effective, less destructive tool. The world always has injustice — proactive change minus the death and carnage. Technology scaling faster than the virtue to wield it, going all the way back to fire.

---

## Segment 4: The Spiral
*How did the problem space compress?*

Talk about: Ukraine war, seeing the future of conflict. Humanity as nature, nature's consolidating brutality. The existential paradox — do nothing or do something. The Einstein haunting — what happens when your imagination has destructive capability. Compressing the philosophy to its lowest form. The answer at maximum reduction: let's not extinct ourselves.

---

## Segment 5: The Irreducible Answer
*Why do you keep going?*

Talk about: love for humanity and fascination with tech. That's it. You can't compress it further. Whatever feels right to close on — where this is heading, what you hope for, what Kannaka means to the mission.
