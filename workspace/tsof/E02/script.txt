Measuring it requires a mathematician's conscience, and the nearest one teaches Tuesday nights at the community college. [CHAUDHARY]

[FLAUKOWSKI]

[CHAUDHARY]

[NARRATOR]

[CHAUDHARY]

Timestamp fabrication — you checked the signature. Aliasing in the sampling — the waveform is captured at four kilohertz, your offset is three hundred forty milliseconds, that is not aliasing, that is a continent. [FLAUKOWSKI]

[CHAUDHARY]

That is the difference between a physicist and a coroner.

[FLAUKOWSKI]

[CHAUDHARY]
